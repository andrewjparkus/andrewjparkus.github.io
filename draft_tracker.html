2026 draft class tracker

Every pick in the 2026 NBA draft, joined to our 2025-26 college / G-League ratings (NBA-equivalent scale). Click a name to open its full multi-league curve in the Explorer — once a prospect debuts in the NBA, the seam and translation are baked in there. Internationals with no college/G-League data are shown greyed ("no pre-draft data"), not hidden. Sort by any column. Descriptive — no projection of how they'll pan out.
net = ORAPM + DRAPM (NBA-equivalent pts/100); college/G-League values are translated to the NBA scale, so a slope <1 means a college number maps to a smaller NBA-scale impact. weff = decay-weighted exposure (low = thin sample, wide CI). The draft→ratings join is name-based and lossy (diacritics/suffixes stripped); accented internationals fall to "no pre-draft data" rather than a wrong join.
