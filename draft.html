Methodology & frozen NCAA→NBA translation

Each estimate translates college / G-League production onto the NBA scale, as of the end of the prospect's final pre-draft season. Intervals are wide and overlap across nearly all prospects: irreducible cross-league uncertainty (slopes < 0.27), not a forecast of NBA careers. This board surfaces calibrated uncertainty, not a confident ranking. Age-driven CI widening is not shipped here (Var_age = 0).

Net = oTS+oTOV+oSC+dTS+dTOV+dSC (NBA-eq). Pick # is the consensus slot; our rank sorts by translated net (no claim of predictive superiority). Badges: thin weff<400 (thin fit support); no data no NCAA/G-League possessions.
