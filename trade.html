Ratings are as-of the snapshot date below — this is a what-if on current season-to-date ratings, not a forecast. Minutes are reallocated pro-rata across the surviving roster (a known simplification for high-usage swaps; flagged when relevant). The 80% band is game-flip sampling noise under a fixed strength shift (it is not rating uncertainty). The residual bar is the part of the strength change not captured by the six factors (per-player RESID and any cold-start fill).
