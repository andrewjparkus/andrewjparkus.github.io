True-talent densities

Every rating here has a confidence interval. Instead of a single point, this plots each player's net as a Gaussian N(net, se²) at their latest game-day. A thin-sample rookie's curve is visibly wide; an established star's is tight. Overlapping curves make "we don't actually know who's better yet" obvious. Add 2–4 players to compare. Descriptive — no projection.
